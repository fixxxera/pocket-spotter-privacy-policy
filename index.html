# Privacy Policy – PocketSpotter **Effective date:** November 5, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to PocketSpotter. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how PocketSpotter handles information when you use our Android application. PocketSpotter is a free application that uses location functionality to provide its core features. ## 2. Information We Collect ### Location Information PocketSpotter may access your device's location when you grant the application location permission. Location information is used solely to provide location-based features and improve your experience within the application. You can control or revoke location permissions at any time through your Android device settings. Some app features may not work without location permission. ### Personal Information PocketSpotter does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or telephone number. ## 3. How We Use Information Location information is used only to: - Provide location-based functionality. - Identify relevant places based on your location. - Improve the functionality of PocketSpotter. ## 4. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information or location information with third parties. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics services. ## 5. Data Storage and Security PocketSpotter does not operate user accounts or maintain a personal user database. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. ## 6. Data Retention PocketSpotter does not maintain a personal user account database or retain location history on its own servers. Location information is used to provide the application's features. Any information stored locally on your device remains subject to your device's settings and storage management. ## 7. Permissions PocketSpotter may request location permissions from Android to provide location-based functionality. You can manage or revoke these permissions through your device settings. ## 8. Children's Privacy PocketSpotter is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. ## 10.
